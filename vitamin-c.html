Vitamin C: Benefits, Food Sources and Supplement Guide

Vitamin C is an important nutrient found naturally in many fruits and vegetables. Learn about its role in normal body functions, dietary sources and what to consider when choosing a vitamin C supplement.

+

Reading time 7 min read

Vitamin C is one of the nutrients that frequently appears in discussions about nutrition and supplementation. It is naturally present in many fruits and vegetables and is also available in dietary supplement products.

What Is Vitamin C?

Vitamin C, also known as ascorbic acid, is a water-soluble vitamin. The body uses vitamin C for several normal biological functions.

Because the human body does not produce vitamin C, it needs to come from food or other dietary sources.

Advertisement

Foods That Contain Vitamin C

A variety of foods can contribute vitamin C to the diet. Fruits and vegetables are particularly useful sources.

  • Oranges and other citrus fruits
  • Guava
  • Peppers
  • Strawberries
  • Tomatoes
  • Broccoli

Why Is Vitamin C Important?

Vitamin C contributes to several normal processes in the body. It is involved in normal collagen formation, contributes to normal immune system function and also contributes to the protection of cells from oxidative stress.

Good nutrition starts with understanding what your body needs and where those nutrients come from.

Vitamin C Supplements

Vitamin C supplements are available in different forms, including tablets, capsules, powders and other formulations.

When considering a supplement, read the product label carefully and follow the manufacturer's directions.

Advertisement

What Should You Consider Before Buying?

Product labels can provide useful information about serving size, ingredients, directions and other product details.

If you take medication, have a medical condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, speak with an appropriate healthcare professional before starting a new supplement.

Food First, Supplements Second

Supplements can be useful in certain situations, but they should not automatically be considered a replacement for a varied and balanced diet.

A healthy eating pattern can provide many different nutrients together with fibre, protein and other components of food.

Key Points

  • Vitamin C is found naturally in many fruits and vegetables.
  • A balanced diet can provide vitamin C.
  • Supplements are available in several forms.
  • Always read supplement labels carefully.
  • Professional advice may be appropriate in certain circumstances.

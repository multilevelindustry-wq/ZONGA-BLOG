Editorial Policy

HealthWise aims to publish useful, responsible and reader-focused health information.

Editorial Independence

Editorial decisions should be based on usefulness, relevance and responsible communication rather than the commercial interests of advertisers or affiliate partners.

Sources and Research

Where appropriate, articles may draw on reputable medical, scientific, governmental, academic and professional sources. Authors should seek to distinguish established evidence from emerging or uncertain information.

Clear Language

We aim to explain health topics in accessible language without unnecessarily sensationalizing medical conditions or promising guaranteed outcomes.

Product Coverage

Product-related content should clearly distinguish factual product information from editorial opinion or promotional material. Affiliate relationships should be disclosed.

Corrections

When a material error is identified, we may correct the article and, when appropriate, clarify what was changed.

Updates

Health information can change. Articles may be reviewed and updated when important information, recommendations or evidence changes.

Privacy Policy

How ZONGO handles information when you use our marketplace.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account or use ZONGO, we may collect information needed to provide marketplace services.

  • Name or business/store name.
  • Email address.
  • Account role such as buyer or seller.
  • Seller product and store information.
  • Seller contact and location information submitted by the seller.
  • Reviews, messages, reports and other information you voluntarily submit.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to create and manage accounts, operate the marketplace, display seller listings, facilitate communication, maintain security and investigate reports or abuse.

3. Seller Information

Sellers may choose to publish information about their store, products, location and contact methods. Information intentionally published as part of a seller listing may be visible to marketplace visitors.

4. Communications

Messages, reviews and reports submitted through ZONGO may be stored so that the marketplace can provide its services, investigate problems and maintain platform safety.

5. Account Security

You are responsible for protecting your login credentials. Do not share your password or verification codes with another person.

6. Third-Party Services

ZONGO may use third-party infrastructure and services to operate features such as authentication, databases, image hosting and communication. Those services may process information according to their own policies.

7. Your Choices

You may contact ZONGO regarding questions about your account or information associated with your use of the marketplace.

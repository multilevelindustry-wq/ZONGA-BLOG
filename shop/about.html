What is ZONGO?

ZONGO is a marketplace platform where sellers can showcase their products and buyers can discover products across different categories and locations.


Our goal is to make product discovery simple while giving sellers an opportunity to build an online presence and giving interested buyers a direct way to communicate with sellers.

🛍️

For Buyers

Discover products, explore seller stores, compare available information, read reviews and contact sellers when you find something interesting.

🏪

For Sellers

Create a seller presence, showcase your products and make it easier for potential customers to discover and contact your business or store.

⭐

What We Value

🔎 Discovery Make it easier for people to find products and sellers.
🤝 Connection Give buyers and sellers a direct way to communicate.
🛡️ Safety Encourage responsible marketplace behaviour and reporting.

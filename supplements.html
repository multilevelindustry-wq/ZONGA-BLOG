About Supplement Products

Supplement information published on HealthWise is provided for general educational purposes. Products should always be used according to their official labels and instructions.

Product availability, ingredients, pricing, packaging and manufacturer information may change. Always review the current information provided by the manufacturer or authorized seller before purchasing.

Supplements are not intended to replace a balanced diet or professional medical care. If you have a medical condition, take medication, are pregnant or breastfeeding, speak with a qualified healthcare professional before using supplements.

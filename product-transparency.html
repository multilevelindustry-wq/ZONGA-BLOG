Product Transparency

At HealthWise, we believe that readers should have confidence in the products and companies featured through our website.

Products From Established Companies

Products featured or linked on HealthWise are sourced from companies, manufacturers, brands, distributors, or sellers that provide information about the products they offer.

Where applicable, products may be manufactured or distributed by companies that operate under relevant industry standards, certifications, registrations, approvals, or regulatory requirements.

We Encourage You to Verify

Product certifications, registrations, approvals, ingredients, availability, prices, and other product information can change over time.

For this reason, we encourage customers to review the manufacturer's official information and product label before making a purchase.

Our Product Links

When you see a product on HealthWise, clicking the product link may take you directly to the company, manufacturer, distributor, or an authorized seller offering that product.

HealthWise does not manufacture every product displayed on the website. The product itself, its packaging, ingredients, instructions, certifications, approvals, warranties, and purchasing terms are determined by the relevant company or seller.

Quality and Transparency

We aim to work with legitimate companies and provide readers with clear information about products rather than misleading claims or promises.

However, the appearance of a product on HealthWise should not be interpreted as a guarantee that the product is suitable for every individual or that it will produce a particular health result.

Before You Purchase

We recommend that customers:

  • Check the manufacturer's official website.
  • Read the product label and ingredients.
  • Check applicable certifications or approvals.
  • Follow the manufacturer's instructions.
  • Check warnings and usage information.
  • Confirm the seller and purchasing terms.

Our Promise to Readers

Our goal is to connect readers with useful information and products from identifiable companies while being transparent about where products come from.

If you believe that information about a product or company displayed on HealthWise is incorrect or outdated, please contact us so that we can review it.

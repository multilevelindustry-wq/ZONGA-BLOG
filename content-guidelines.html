Content Guidelines

HealthWise is committed to publishing health and wellness content that is useful, responsible and respectful.

Accuracy

Authors should avoid unsupported medical claims, fabricated statistics and misleading statements. Important factual claims should be checked against appropriate sources.

No Guaranteed Cures

Content must not promise guaranteed cures, miracle results or guaranteed health outcomes.

Respectful Language

We avoid stigmatizing, discriminatory, unnecessarily frightening or sensational language when discussing health conditions or people.

Product Claims

Product descriptions should not exaggerate benefits or imply that a supplement can replace professional medical care.

Images

Images should be relevant and presented in a way that does not intentionally mislead readers about a product, condition or health outcome.

Corrections

Readers can report suspected errors through our Contact Us page. Submissions should identify the relevant article and explain the concern clearly.

Commercial Transparency

Affiliate links, advertisements and sponsored content should be clearly distinguished from independent editorial information.
